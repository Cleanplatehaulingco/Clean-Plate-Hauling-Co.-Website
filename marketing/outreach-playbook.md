## 2. Do These First (this week, in order)

### A. Google Business Profile — your #1 job source
Most junk removal jobs come from someone Googling "junk removal near me."
This is free and beats any cold outreach in volume.

1. Go to business.google.com → create profile for "Clean Plate Hauling Co."
2. Set it as a **service-area business** (hide your home address, list your 12 cities)
3. Category: "Junk removal service" (primary), add "Debris removal service", "Garbage collection service"
4. Add 10+ photos: your white Sierra 3500HD, you working, before/afters
5. Hours 7am–8pm daily, phone 734-743-1877, link the website
6. **AI prompt:** paste the Master Prompt, then: *"Write my Google Business
   Profile description, under 750 characters."* and *"Write 4 weekly Google
   Business posts for this month, one per service."* Post one every week — it keeps you ranking.

### B. Make the truck a billboard
A white 3500HD is a rolling ad. Get magnetic door signs or vinyl lettering:
**JUNK REMOVAL • Veteran Owned • 734-743-1877** — big enough to read at a stoplight.
Park it visibly (busy lots, your driveway facing the road). Cheapest leads you'll ever get.

### C. Review engine (start at job #1)
Reviews are what make Google and customers trust a new company. After EVERY job,
text this before you leave the driveway:

> Thanks again for choosing Clean Plate Hauling! If you have 60 seconds, a Google
> review helps my small veteran-owned business more than you know: [your review link]

Get your review link: Google Business Profile → "Ask for reviews" → copy short URL.
Goal: 10 reviews in 30 days. Discounting a job $20 in exchange for nothing but
*asking* nicely is worth it early on.

### D. Speed-to-lead
Junk removal goes to whoever answers first. Set this missed-call auto-text on your
phone (iPhone: Settings → Phone → Respond with Text; or carrier/Google Voice feature):

> This is Clean Plate Hauling — sorry I missed you, I'm probably on a job.
> Text me photos of what you need gone and I'll send you a firm flat-rate
> price within the hour. — 734-743-1877

## 7. Paid Channels (when cash flow allows, in this order)

1. **Thumbtack** — pay per lead, works immediately, builds early reviews. Start with
   a small weekly budget; respond within minutes or don't bother.
2. **Google Local Services Ads** — "Google Guaranteed" badge, pay per *lead* not click.
   Needs license/insurance docs + background check. Turn on once you have 5+ reviews;
   this is the big one for junk removal.
3. Skip Angi for now (expensive, shared leads). Yard signs at busy corners are cheap
   if local ordinances allow.

## 8. Track It (or it didn't happen)

Use `marketing/lead-tracker.csv` (starter targets already filled in). Every Friday,
tally: outreach touches · quotes sent · jobs booked · revenue · new reviews.
Paste the numbers into AI with the Master Prompt and ask what to adjust.

**The math:** 10 B2B touches/day ≈ 200/month → expect 5–10 conversations → 2–3
recurring accounts. Two property managers + one estate sale company = a base of
steady monthly work while Google reviews compound. Stay on it for 90 days.
