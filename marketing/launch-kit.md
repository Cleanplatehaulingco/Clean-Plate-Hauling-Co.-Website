## 6. CRM Setup — Pipeline That Books Jobs

Create these **stages** (works in any CRM — tell me which one you use and I'll map it exactly):

1. **New Lead** — anything that comes in (form, text, call, FB, Thumbtack)
2. **Contacted** — you replied (goal: under 5 minutes, every time)
3. **Quoted** — firm price sent · *set automatic follow-up task for +24h if no answer*
4. **Scheduled** — date/time booked · send reminder text day before
5. **Completed** — job done & paid
6. **Review Requested** — review text sent (same day as job!)
7. **Repeat/Referral pool** — B2B accounts & happy customers · check in quarterly

**Log on every lead:** name, phone, city, source (Google/FB/Thumbtack/referral — so you learn what's working), items, quote amount, photos.

**Two non-negotiable automations** (or phone reminders if your CRM can't):
- New lead older than 5 minutes without reply → alert
- Quote older than 24h without answer → follow-up task: "Hi [name], still want that [couch] gone? I have [day] open. — Clean Plate Hauling"

**Friday numbers (5 min):** leads in → quotes sent → jobs booked → revenue → reviews. Paste into AI with the Master Prompt and ask what to adjust.
